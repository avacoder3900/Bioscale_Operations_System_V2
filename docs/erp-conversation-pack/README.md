# ERP Conversation Pack

Three docs to prep for the conversation with your finance-experienced superior about the BIMS financial layer. Read them in order.

## 01 — Conversation prep

The plain-English memo. Walks through what we're doing, the vocabulary in flowing prose, where our thinking is, what you actually need from your exec, and how to run the meeting. Read this first. ~1,800 words, comfortable read.

## 02 — Questions

The formal question list with severity tags. Use this during the meeting to walk through item by item. Eleven main questions plus five bonus ones. Each has the question, why it matters, the engineering default, and the typical answer pattern.

## 03 — Primer

The term-by-term reference. Every finance term explained in 1–2 sentences with engineer-friendly intuition. Use this as a refresher right before the meeting, or to dig deeper on a specific concept.

## Recommended flow

1. **Day before:** Read 01 end-to-end. Skim 03 for any term that's unfamiliar.
2. **Hour before:** Skim 03 again, focus on the five strategic questions in 01.
3. **In the meeting:** Open 02 on a tablet or print it. Use it to keep the conversation moving.
4. **After:** Paste the answers into the next chat session and we'll fold them into the master plan.
